The Beginning

Brush Up Stores was a DIY project from the very beginning.

This is how Brush Up Stores started. Originally I purchased this property where the interior and exterior were both in need of drastic renovation. After that, I got to work!

I had to take out a lot of parts including the walls and floors.

Now we have a comfortable haircut environment!

There was a lot of space in the back of the 1st floor that needed fixing. I also wondered how I could make the shop comfortable for customers with kids.

The waiting area!

Kids have a place to play while they wait!

The corridor needed better flooring and styling.

Now it has a simple yet attractive design

Here is the entrance, I hoped that customers can get a whole view of the store after walking in. Payments are made easily at the self-register.

It took a while, but now I am proud to call this place my first barbershop!
